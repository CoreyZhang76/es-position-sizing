What works

1
The vol forecaster. Ranks the next few seconds calm-vs-busy at AUC 0.83–0.90 on real ES, always out-of-sample. Most of the power is HAR; order flow adds a real but modest short-horizon gain. This is the core asset.
2
Execution timing — the deployable edge. Trading in the forecast-calm third of windows cuts the volatility traded through by ~29% (band 23–34%): about 0.8 bps/window less timing risk, and ~29% lower market impact if child orders are sized to volume (POV) — a fixed clip into thinner calm windows raises impact instead.
3
VRP harvesting — a real but modest premium. The variance risk premium is large on our data (+6.2 vol points, positive 91% of days). A roll-aware short-vol carry nets ~0.31 Sharpe with −27% drawdowns after vol-targeting. A harvestable risk premium, not a high-Sharpe alpha — and it shares equities' crash, so it does not diversify a long book.

What we tested and dropped — honestly

×
Direction is not tradable. Flow predicts the next move's sign strongly out-of-sample, but the edge is sub-spread — net of the bid-ask it is about zero.
×
Sizing is not alpha. Vol-targeting does not beat fixed on Sharpe at matched risk (on ES the high-vol days pay); it is drawdown / risk control. Conditional de-risking keeps Sharpe and cuts the tail.
×
Cross-asset VIX adds nothing to short-horizon ES vol forecasting (level, change, and flow all null on this window). And dispersion / VIX sector rotation are not runnable with our data and infrastructure.

Where it stands

Deployable now

  • The vol forecaster on ES, on the CME feed we already have.
  • A forecast-calm execution scheduler, POV-sized, to cut timing risk and impact.

Needs more

  • Book-replay sim now runs on real L10 data: calm-tilting is a net execution win for small parents (≲0.5% of daily volume); larger orders must spread over more days.
  • Real desk execution logs, or a desk-calibrated impact model (cross-team).
  • Cross-regime validation on more feature-store months; NQ / ZN cross-asset flow.

The honest framing

The value is execution and risk management on a working volatility forecast — not a new directional alpha. That is a smaller claim than “we found alpha,” and it is one the data supports end to end.
