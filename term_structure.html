The VIX term structure trade

Short the front of the VIX curve and hold a later month against it. That earns the volatility premium with far less of the crash than an outright short. Drag the hedge to see the crash come out, then turn on "size to a risk target" to run it as a book.

← Overview  ·  Position sizing  ·  Volatility forecaster

What this is: a candidate crash-hedged way to be short volatility, and a new volatility signal (the curve's slope and premium) that a price-direction model does not capture. A research prototype on real out-of-sample data, not a live strategy.
